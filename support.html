II. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Why can't the APP find my LED display device?

Please check the following:

  • Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone (Settings → Bluetooth).
  • Grant Bluetooth permission to LEDdisplay when prompted.
  • Make sure the LED display device is powered on and not connected to another phone.
  • Keep your phone within 5 meters of the LED display device.

Q2: Why is the connection unstable or frequently disconnects?

Possible causes and solutions:

  • Stay close to the LED display device to avoid signal interference.
  • Move away from other Bluetooth or Wi-Fi devices that may cause interference.
  • Restart the LED display device and try connecting again.
  • Make sure the LED display device has sufficient battery.

Q3: Why is the content I sent not showing on the LED display?

Please try the following:

  • Reconnect the device and re-send the content.
  • Check that the content does not exceed the LED display's supported resolution or character limit.
  • Make sure the LED display's display mode is set to receive content from the APP.

Q4: Does the APP support iPad?

Yes, LEDdisplay supports both iPhone and iPad. On iPad, you can take advantage of the larger screen to design and edit your display content more conveniently.

Q5: Will my data be lost after I delete the APP?

All display content you create is stored locally on your device. Once you delete the APP, this data will be cleared. We recommend backing up important content before deleting the APP.

Q6: How do I report a bug or request a feature?

We welcome your feedback! Please send an email to office1889@163.com with a description of the problem or feature suggestion. If it is a bug, please attach relevant screenshots or screen recordings so we can resolve the issue faster.

III. Before Contacting Us

To help us resolve your issue more efficiently, please include the following information in your email:

  • App version (Settings screen of the APP)
  • iOS / iPadOS version
  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Pro)
  • LED display device model (if applicable)
  • A description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot or screen recording

IV. Compatible Devices

LEDdisplay requires iOS 14.0 or later, and supports iPhone and iPad. The APP communicates with LED display devices via Bluetooth and is compatible with most LED display devices that support the standard BLE protocol. For specific device compatibility, please contact us via email.
