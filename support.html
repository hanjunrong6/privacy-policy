About LEDwriter

LEDwriter is a mobile application that allows you to create and display text, graphics, and animations on LED display devices via Bluetooth. The app supports multiple languages, custom fonts, and various animation effects, making it easy to personalize your LED display content.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Cannot connect to the LED device?

Please ensure that: (1) Bluetooth is enabled on your phone; (2) The LED device is powered on and within range; (3) Bluetooth permission has been granted to the app. If the problem persists, try restarting both the app and the LED device.

2. The display content is not updating?

Make sure the Bluetooth connection is stable. If the connection is lost, the app will automatically attempt to reconnect. You can also manually refresh the connection in the device settings.

3. App language is not correct?

LEDwriter supports multiple languages. The app will automatically match your device's system language. You can also manually switch languages in the app settings.

4. How to use custom fonts?

You can add custom font files in the app's font settings. Supported font formats include .fnt.bin files. Once added, you can select them in the text editor.
